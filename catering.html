02 — What we bring

Dishes made for sharing

Catering is priced by tray and number of guests. Tell us about your event and we'll send a menu and a quote, usually the same day.

Kazan plov Палоо

The heart of every Kyrgyz celebration: rice slow-cooked in the kazan with beef, sweet carrots, garlic and cumin, with boiled eggs and achichuk salad.

Manti trays Манты

Hand-folded lamb and onion dumplings, steamed and packed hot, with our house chili.

Shashlik platters Шашлык

Marinated lamb and chicken skewers grilled over charcoal, with onions, herbs and bread.

Lagman Лагман

Homemade pulled noodles with beef and vegetables. Boso is wok-tossed, gyuro comes with a rich gravy.

Samsa & salads Самса

Flaky baked samsa with lamb or chicken and cheese, plus fresh achichuk and garden salads to round out the table.
